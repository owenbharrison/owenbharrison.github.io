Usage

• Drag the left cursor to emit particles.
• Drag the right cursor to emit rainbow particles.
• Press the middle cursor to emit a bomb of particles.
• Press E to toggle the emitters position to the cursor.
• Press S to toggle simple sprite rendering.
• Press P to pause/play the simulation.

Overview

This project uses a simple particle system of rotated rectangles. Each particle is spawned with an initial velocity, size, rotation, lifespan, and age. Over time, the rectangles rise with buoyancy, grow, and spin slower. Their relative life is factored into their transparency. This makes the effect convincing. Once a particle's age is greater than its lifespan, it is removed.

Possible Use Case

This project could be used in game development as a steam/smog effect.
